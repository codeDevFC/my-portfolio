On Sunday, I talked about eternal life. On Monday, I talk about `npm install` failing.

Honestly? `npm` errors have tested my faith more than any theological debate ever did.

But here's the crossover: Both require you to take a deep breath, read the error message carefully (the "scripture" of the terminal), and trust that the fix is usually simpler than you think.

If you can survive a church council meeting, you can survive a merge conflict. I promise.
