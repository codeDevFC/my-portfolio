There are two types of leaders: Shepherds and Sheepdogs.

Sheepdogs bark and herd from behind. Shepherds walk in front and lead to green pastures.

In frontend, too many "lead" devs are just sheepdogs – they chase tickets and bark about deadlines. A pastoral lead dev walks ahead, envisions the architecture, and protects the team from burnout.

Lead with vision, not velocity. Your team's mental health is more important than your DORA metrics.

**Reflection:** What kind of leader are you? What kind do you want to become?
