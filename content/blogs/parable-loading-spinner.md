A user waits 3 seconds for a page to load. If they see a blank screen, they feel abandoned. If they see a spinner, they feel hope.

In pastoral care, presence matters. Even if you don't have the answer yet, you show up. In frontend, skeleton screens and optimistic UI do the same thing – they tell the user, "I hear you, I'm working on it."

Don't leave your users in existential darkness. Give them feedback. Always.

**Reflection:** How does your application communicate with users during waiting states? Are you giving them hope or abandonment?
