The Product Manager says, "Can we just change the font size?"

The pastoral training says, "What is the *real* need behind that request?"

Often, they don't want a bigger font. They want the client to feel heard. So you build a quick prototype to validate the feeling, rather than wasting 3 days on a design system change.

Ministry taught me to listen for the unspoken prayer. Frontend taught me to code the spoken requirements. The magic happens in the gap between them.

**Reflection:** What's a request you've received recently? What was the real need behind it?
