Confession: I once spent 4 hours debugging a bug that was caused by a missing closing `</div>`.

In ministry, I used to tell people, "You are forgiven." I had to learn to say that to myself in code.

The tech industry worships geniuses. But the best devs I know are the ones who admit they don't know, ask for help, and fix the typo without shame.

Grace isn't just for the pulpit. It's for the pull request.

**Reflection:** What's a bug that humbled you? How did you grow from it?
