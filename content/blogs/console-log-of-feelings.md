In JavaScript, `console.log()` shows you exactly what's happening. In human interactions, we call that "active listening."

Most devs debug code with breakpoints. Few debug user frustration with patience.

In my pastoral years, I learned that a user's rage-click isn't a bug report; it's a cry for help. As a frontend dev, I don't just fix the UI. I fix the UX of trust.

Write code that doesn't make people feel stupid. That's the full-stack of empathy.

**Reflection:** How can you listen more deeply to your users today? What are they really telling you?
