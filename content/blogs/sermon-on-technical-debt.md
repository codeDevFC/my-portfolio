I used to preach about sin. Now I preach about `useEffect` dependency arrays.

Both cause chaos if you ignore them.

Technical debt is like unresolved guilt – it compounds interest. You can ignore it for 6 sprints, but eventually, the whole system crashes on a Sunday evening.

Pastoral training taught me to address issues early, gently, and transparently. Code reviews aren't interrogations; they are confessional booths for better logic.

Handle your state management like you handle your relationships – with clarity and zero side-effects.

**Reflection:** What technical debt are you ignoring today? What would happen if you addressed it now?
