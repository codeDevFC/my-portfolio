A church without a ramp excludes the disabled. A website without semantic HTML excludes the blind.

In ministry, we called it "radical hospitality." In tech, we call it "a11y."

If your `<div>` soup ignores screen readers, you are literally locking people out of the digital sanctuary.

As a pastoral dev, I don't see accessibility as a checklist. I see it as a moral imperative. Make the web inclusive. Your `aria-label` might be someone's only way in.

**Reflection:** What's one thing you can do today to make your code more accessible?
